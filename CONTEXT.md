# Seenmark

Seenmark is how an adult in the United States tracks their own hair thinning and chooses a next step. A clinic is the last step, not the front door.

## Language

**Member**:
An adult in the United States who uses Seenmark to understand their own hair thinning and choose a next step.
_Avoid_: User, patient, customer, lead

**Clinic**:
A hair restoration practice a member may later be introduced to. A clinic is not a member.
_Avoid_: Provider, doctor, vendor, seller
