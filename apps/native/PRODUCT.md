## Platform

The Expo app renders with each platform's own toolkit through Expo UI: SwiftUI on iOS and Jetpack Compose (Material 3) on Android (`README.md`, "Native app UI"). Its palette mirrors the web app's (`lib/constants.ts`).

## Users, purpose and constraints

Same as the root `PRODUCT.md`. Native-specific: check-ins come from the camera only, never the photo library. Navigation is native tabs (check-ins, account) with a native stack per tab.
