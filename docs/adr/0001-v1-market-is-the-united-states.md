# The v1 market is the United States

The brief prices the decision in dollars and compares a flight to Istanbul with a US clinic bill, and it borrows NerdWallet and the Better Business Bureau for what Seenmark is. The v1 member is an adult in the United States. A clinic is not a member. Other countries are a later product, not a setting on this one.

India was the real alternative, because that is where the work is being started, and so was a country-agnostic first version. Both were rejected. The search, the price gap, and the trust analogies that justify the product are American. A global shell would hide the regulatory and supply decisions instead of making them.
