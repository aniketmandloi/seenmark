# The member never pays, and a clinic does not pay in v1

Seenmark stays free to the member. There is no subscription, and there is no cut of a surgery fee. A clinic does not pay in v1 either. The introduction is recorded so we can see demand. Charging a clinic for it waits until verified means something and a clinic actually exists.

The tempting alternative was to turn on clinic fees immediately, because that is the business in the brief, or to put affiliate links on every band so the app earns before any clinic joins. Clinic fees with an empty network are a price list for a product we cannot deliver. Affiliates on every band would make the band look purchased. Whether a single disclosed link is allowed on a menu at all waits on the legal boundary, and is not decided here.
