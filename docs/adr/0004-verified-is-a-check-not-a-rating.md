# Verified is a check, not a rating

Nobody selling trust in US hair restoration publishes an ongoing, comparable, independent score of clinic results at 12 months. RealSelf verifies licenses, stars, and reviews. ISHRS, ABHRS, and IAHRS sell credentials. Hair Transplant Network sells a sponsor listing. Bosley's 12-month rule is its own warranty. Hims, Keeps, and Ro cite drug trials. Seenmark will not reuse any of those as the meaning of verified.

A verified clinic is one Seenmark has checked: a named physician with an unrestricted US license, a price range published to the member before an introduction, and any result photo shown through Seenmark taken at least 12 months later, of that physician's patient, and not taken from the clinic's ads. Membership in a society can be stated as a fact. It is not the check. There is no star rating and no "guaranteed results."

v1 does not deliver an introduction to a clinic. The request is stored. Delivery starts only after a clinic has passed this check. Running our own outcome studies stays out of scope, so v1 does not rank clinics against each other.

The brief asked to rate clinics on 12-month results. A public ranking was rejected because we would be inventing a measurement none of the neighbors have, and calling it verified before a single clinic has been checked. The 12-month rule survives as a filter on photos, which is the part a member can actually see.
