# A paid link cannot move the band

FTC endorsement rules allow a disclosed commission next to an independent recommendation. They do not allow payment to decide a ranking, and a disclosure does not cure that. "Paid link" has to sit next to the link. "Affiliate link" alone is not enough.

v1 may put one paid link on the early menu, off the screen where the member chooses the band, labeled "Paid link", with no hair-growth claim. The member's band is already chosen before that page, and who pays cannot change the band or the order of the list. The mid menu and the late menu have no paid link. A clinic cannot buy placement. v1 can still ship with no paid link at all.
