# The NerdWallet of hair clinics

## The problem

823,000 people a month search "hair loss treatment." Up 124% this year and climbing.

Everyone knows a guy who flew to Istanbul to save cash. $2,000 vs $15,000. I get it, but I've never been to Turkey. Imagine flying blind to get surgery.

## The product

The Better Business Bureau of hair clinics, with an app as the front door. Photo-track your hairline, catch it early, get a score. The score tells you what to do next, and the verified clinic network is the last stop.

### The path

- **A little thinning.** You get pointed at the oils and habits. Cheap fixes first to build the trust.
- **Further along.** The medicines and vitamins worth asking about. Not medical advice, just the menu.
- **Point of no return.** You get matched to a verified clinic network. Rated on 12-month results, not marketing photos.

## The business

The app is free. It's the lead magnet. Affiliate money on the oils and the telehealth scripts as people's condition advances.

Verified clinics pay to be in the network, because the app hands them a buyer at the exact moment they've decided it's time for treatment. Highest-intent lead in the category.
